  • Administration: Take with or after food to minimize transient GI side effects (nausea, diarrhoea, abdominal pain).
  • Formulation: Consider slow-release (MR/XR) once daily if GI side effects persist or to improve adherence.
  • Lifestyle: Always combine with structured diet and physical activity interventions.

Personalized Monitoring Plan

  • Baseline (Pre-treatment): Renal function (eGFR), baseline weight, BMI, HbA1c, and lipids.
  • Ongoing Clinical Review: Weight/BMI trajectory and screen for lactic acidosis symptoms (dyspnoea, muscle cramps, abdominal pain, hypothermia, asthenia).
  • Metabolic & Hepatic: Liver function tests (LFTs) and HbA1c annually.
  • Renal Function (eGFR): Annually Standard monitoring interval.
  • Vitamin B12 Levels: Annually Standard monitoring interval (treat deficiency per local guidelines).
  • Sick Day Rule (Temporary Hold): Stop immediately if systemically unwell with dehydration (severe diarrhoea/vomiting, fever, reduced fluid intake) or severe infection. Restart once well and hydrated.
  • Lactic Acidosis / Acute Organ Failure: Stop immediately if lactic acidosis is suspected, or with acute alcohol intoxication, decompensated heart failure, respiratory failure, recent MI, or shock.
  • Iodinated Contrast Imaging: Discontinue prior to or at the time of contrast administration; restart ≥48 hours later only after renal function is re-evaluated and stable.
  • Permanent Discontinuation Criteria: Stop if eGFR drops <30 mL/min, if BMI falls <20 kg/m², or if the antipsychotic is discontinued.
